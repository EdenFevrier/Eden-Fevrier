Philosophy of Teaching

My pedagogical approach is rooted in the belief that language learning is both a rigorous structural discipline and a dynamic cultural exchange. Having earned an advanced certification in artificial intelligence for teaching and learning, I actively integrate emerging technologies into the classroom to create adaptive, highly engaging linguistic environments for my students.
