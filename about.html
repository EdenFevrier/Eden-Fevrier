About Me

Academic Background

I am a fourth-year doctoral candidate in linguistics based in Amherst, NY. My research operates at the intersection of morphosemantics and syntax, with a dedicated focus on creole languages. Specifically, I analyze the derivational morphology of Haitian Creole to better understand complex structural dependencies and semantic representations.

Professional Practice

Beyond academia, I serve as the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Garden of Languages and Services (GOLS), an enterprise offering professional translation, interpretation, localization, linguistic consulting, and language instruction. As a sworn translator, I ensure precise, contextually nuanced communication across critical linguistic borders.

I also direct LINGUISTICA LAB, an educational digital brand aimed at disseminating academic scholarship, linguistics research, and communication strategies to a broader audience.

Linguistic Profile

I possess native/fluent proficiency in French and Haitian Creole. As a lifelong language learner and educator, I actively study and teach a diverse portfolio of languages, including Spanish, Catalan, Mandarin Chinese, Italian, and Portuguese.
